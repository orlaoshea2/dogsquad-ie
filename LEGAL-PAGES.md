# Legal pages added

Added production routes:
- `/privacy-policy`
- `/terms`

Both are linked from the site-wide footer and the account sign-in screen. The sitemap now uses `https://dogsquad.ie` and includes both legal pages.

The wording is based on the application's current source, including account/authentication, Google sign-in, bookings, consultation messages, Cloudflare hosting, Lovable Cloud/Supabase backend, browser storage, and the payment flow. Business/legal details that are not present in the source have not been invented.

Before submitting the Google OAuth app for production verification, the business owner should review the pages and confirm the controller/legal business name, contact details, retention practices, cancellation/refund policy, and any other business-specific legal requirements.

This document is an implementation note, not legal advice.
